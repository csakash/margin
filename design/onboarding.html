The quiet power of writing in the margins

Every serious reader eventually discovers that the best ideas show up mid-paragraph. Not before you start, not after you finish, but right in the middle of someone else's sentence.

Most note tools ask you to leave. Switch apps, find the right notebook, wait for a sync spinner, and by the time the cursor blinks the thought has already gone soft. A margin never asks you to leave.

It sits right next to the text, patient and empty, ready the moment something sparks.
